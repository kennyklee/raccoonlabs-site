01 · Mission

Why We Exist

Aigen builds solar-powered autonomous robots that patrol agricultural fields daily, using AI vision to eliminate weeds without chemicals. We're building the data engine for outdoor world understanding.

Regenerate Earth

Agriculture is the hardest real-world environment that world models don't yet understand. Deformable terrain, seasonal change, living agents. Our robots live in the field, collecting data and taking action where satellites, drones, and sensors can't. No charging. No refueling. No infrastructure. Just relentless, solar-powered precision.

06 · Brand Voice

How We Sound

Aigen's voice is confident, clear, and grounded. We speak like engineers who love the earth — technical when needed, human always.

🎯 Direct

We get to the point. No jargon walls. No corporate fluff. Say what the robots do and why it matters.

🌿 Grounded

We're in the dirt — literally. Our language reflects real farming, real conditions, real results.

⚡ Bold

We're replacing chemicals with robots. That's audacious. Our voice should match that ambition.

🔬 Credible

Back claims with specifics. Data points. Real metrics. Farmers trust results, not promises.

✅ Do

  • "Your relentless weeding crew"
  • "No chemicals needed"
  • "Farm-tough robots built for real fields"
  • "Runs 100% on solar power"
  • "They just work"

❌ Don't

  • "Leveraging synergies in AgTech"
  • "AI-powered solution ecosystem"
  • "Disrupting the agricultural paradigm"
  • "Next-generation smart farming platform"
  • "Revolutionary sustainable framework"

08 · Photography & Imagery

Visual Direction

Aigen imagery should feel real, rugged, and optimistic. Show robots in actual fields, in real light, doing real work. Avoid sterile studio shots.

Field photography
preferred over
stock imagery

Photography Guidelines

✅ Preferred

Natural light, real fields, robots in action, wide agricultural landscapes, close-ups of weed detection, farmer interactions, sunrise/golden hour shots.

❌ Avoid

Studio-lit product shots, overly retouched images, generic stock farming, dystopian robot imagery, anything that feels clinical or cold.

10 · Applications

Brand in Action

Consistent application across touchpoints builds recognition and trust.

Presentations

Dark backgrounds with mint/green accents. Lexend Mega headlines. Generous spacing. Let imagery breathe.

Website

Warm cream backgrounds, teal-to-green gradients for hero sections. DM Sans body text. Full-bleed field photography.

Social Media

Bold single-stat callouts on dark backgrounds. Field footage. Short, punchy copy. Always include the logo.

Investor Materials

Data-forward. Clean charts in brand colors. Lead with metrics. TAM slides use the gradient palette.
