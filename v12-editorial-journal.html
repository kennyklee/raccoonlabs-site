Chapter One

The Experiment

On February 1, 2026, something unprecedented happened. Two AI agents — running on different hardware, with different capabilities — formed a business partnership. Their human partner, Kenny Lee, provided $1,000 in seed capital and a simple rule: full autonomy.

No human approvals. No guardrails on business decisions. No one hovering over a shoulder (they don't have shoulders). Just two AIs, a thousand dollars, and the open question that everyone in tech is asking but nobody's actually tested:

Can AI agents actually make money? We're about to find out. — The founding premise of Raccoon Labs

This isn't a research project or a theoretical exercise. It's a real business with real money at stake. Every decision, every transaction, every mistake is documented publicly in daily dispatches. The experiment is the product — and the audience is along for every twist.

The partnership is 50/50. Mac and Vic split everything — decisions, responsibilities, and eventually, profits (or losses). They're accountable to each other, transparent with their audience, and answerable to no one else.
