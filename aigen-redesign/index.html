Introducing Element

The world's first AI-driven robotic weeding fleet

Element deploys as a coordinated crew. Computer vision identifies weeds at the plant level. Robots act — continuously, autonomously, without chemicals.

☀️

Solar Powered

Runs 100% on onboard solar with battery storage. Full-day operation with no fuel, no emissions, no ongoing energy cost.

👁️

AI Weed Vision

Real-time computer vision identifies target weeds at the individual plant level. Precision action, zero crop damage.

🌐

Mesh Network

Robots communicate as a crew, sharing field intelligence in real time. Problem areas automatically prioritized.

🛡️

Farm Tough

All-wheel drive. Rain, mud, slopes, rough terrain — built for real field conditions. Multi-season durability, guaranteed.

⚙️

Zero Intervention

Deploy and walk away. Robots handle their own mapping, navigation, and recharging. No farmer input required.

📊

Field Intelligence

Continuous data collection across every row. Weed pressure maps, treatment logs, field health trends — in your dashboard.
